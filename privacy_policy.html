Privacy Policy & Terms of Service — VD Pro

Effective Date: October 26, 2026

Application: VD Pro (Package: com.elhafidy.vdpro)

Developer: Elhafidy

Summary: VD Pro is a privacy-first web browser and download utility. We do NOT collect, transmit, store, sell, or share any personal data, browsing history, or media files. All data remains exclusively on your personal device.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

Unlike other applications, VD Pro operates entirely client-side on your Android device:

  • No Personal Information: We do not ask for or collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or account credentials.
  • No Browsing History: The internal browser processes web pages locally. Your browsing activity, visited URLs, and search queries are never transmitted to any external server.
  • No Tracking & No Analytics: VD Pro contains zero third-party advertising SDKs, behavioral tracking scripts, or analytics frameworks (such as Firebase Analytics, Facebook SDK, or adjust).

2. Device Permissions Used

VD Pro requests only the strictly necessary Android permissions required to perform its core functionality:

  • android.permission.INTERNET: Required to access public websites and download media files that you choose.
  • android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Used to determine if Wi-Fi or mobile data is connected (to support the "Download on Wi-Fi only" user setting).
  • android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS: (Android 13+) Used exclusively to display download progress and completion notifications.
  • android.permission.FOREGROUND_SERVICE & FOREGROUND_SERVICE_DATA_SYNC: Allows downloads to continue smoothly in the background without being abruptly terminated by the operating system.

3. File Storage & Scoped Storage Compliance

In full compliance with Android 10+ Scoped Storage requirements:

  • Downloaded files are stored in the application's private external storage or standard Downloads directory.
  • VD Pro does not request broad READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE or MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE permissions.
  • You can delete or manage any downloaded file at any time directly through the app or your device's file manager.

4. Terms of Service & Google Play Compliance

  • Authorized Use: VD Pro is intended for downloading media content that you personally own or have explicit legal permission to download from the content creator or host.
  • Copyright Respect: Users must respect intellectual property rights. The unauthorized downloading of copyrighted or protected material is strictly prohibited.
  • Google Play Developer Policy: This application adheres to Google Play Developer Program Policies. The application does not promote or encourage the violation of third-party terms of service.
